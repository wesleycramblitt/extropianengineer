Extropian Synthesis is the ecosystem's generative design platform: instead of generating imagery or text, it synthesizes *machines* — combining known laws of nature, geometries, materials, and computational algorithms to invent novel designs at any scale.

The premise is that generated concepts are only useful when they can be simulated and validated. Synthesis is being built on the Extropian simulation stack so that every proposed design is checkable against real physics.
