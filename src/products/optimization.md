Optimization turns engineering judgment loops into automated search. Extropian Optimization is a modular engine for driving simulations and analyses toward better designs with less manual effort — exploring design spaces that would be impractical to cover by hand.

Its modular design means the optimization layer can wrap your existing solver rather than requiring you to migrate to a new one.
