Complex systems are hard to communicate. Extropian Composer is built for context-aware, interactive visualization of complex topics or systems — letting viewers explore relationships, states, and behavior instead of staring at static diagrams.

It shares its rendering and interaction foundation with the engineering tools, so a Composer view can sit on top of real simulation or sensor data rather than being a stylized mock.
