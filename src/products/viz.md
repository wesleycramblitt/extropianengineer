Solver output is only valuable when it can be seen, compared, and understood. Extropian Viz covers the post-processing side of the simulation loop — turning raw results into interactive visualizations engineers can explore and compare. It is being built on the same modern rendering stack as the rest of the ecosystem rather than on aging visualization toolkits.
