What I Do

I help engineering and research teams turn complex models and real-world data into fast, interactive design and digital-twin systems that shorten development cycles, improve decisions, and optimize physical systems throughout their lifecycle.

Make Complex Results Interactive

I build interactive 2D and 3D tools that help engineers explore results, compare designs, adjust parameters, and communicate complex system behavior more clearly.

Modernize Proven Scientific Software

I modernize validated scientific and engineering software so it becomes easier to maintain, extend, deploy, and commercialize — without replacing proven models or starting over.

Simplify Complex Simulation Workflows

I build the software around scientific models and solvers—wrapping existing tools with clean APIs, connecting disconnected components, and automating setup, execution, data handling, and post-processing so engineers can run complex studies through one coherent workflow.

Originating in the 1990s, it required modernization, customization, and deployment to Sandia National Laboratories. Building an Interactive Engineering Interface Around a Hypersonic Adaptive-Mesh Solver

HyperFat is a CFD solver with adaptive mesh refinement for hypersonic flows. The underlying solver was powerful but inaccessible to non-developers. I am building a commercial-grade interactive UI/UX — editing models and boundary conditions, managing batch processes, save/load scenes with reproducibility, and a command-bus architecture separating the UI from computational operations. Active contract with Whoosh HPC Lab LLC.

Unifying Fragmented RNA Analysis Into an End-to-End Research Platform

Autoligo takes researchers from raw sequencing data through downstream analysis, annotation, and interpretation — replacing a patchwork of disconnected tools with a single reproducible platform. Built in collaboration with Kansas University graduate students, the system reduces manual analysis burden and makes non-coding RNA research accessible to domain scientists rather than only programmers.

Sustaining and Modernizing a Mission-Critical Emergency Response Simulator

AlphaACT is an emergency-response training platform used by government and enterprise clients. Since 2018 I have served as the primary developer — maintaining, upgrading, and customizing the system for multiple deployments. The engagement spans legacy code stewardship, feature development, client-specific customization, and ongoing technical support across years.

“Wes is extremely proficient, diligent and highly competent. He quickly understands a task, finds effective solutions to simple or complex problems and implements on time and within budget. What more could you ask!” — Reed Hodgin, AlphaACT

IoT Fleet-Managed Sensor Platform on Raspberry Pi

Solvere is an Electron-based application deployed on Raspberry Pi devices, interfacing directly with PID controllers, sensors, and actuators for real-time liquid chromatography detection. The fleet was managed and deployed remotely using Balena OS for over-the-air updates, health monitoring, and centralized fleet management across distributed hardware installations.

Engagement Models

Every engagement starts with a free 15-minute fit call to discuss your system and goals. If there is a match, I recommend one of the paths below.

Technical Assessment

A fixed-scope paid audit of your existing system. You receive: findings, risks, options, estimated impact, and recommended next steps — not just a conversation.

Fixed-Scope Modernization Sprint

Time-boxed engagements to upgrade, optimize, fix critical bugs, or build something new. Scoped to a clear deliverable, budgeted as a fixed price based on value.

Embedded Specialist Engagement

Short- to medium-term integration with your team as a hands-on technical contributor — simulation, GPU compute, visualization, or legacy modernization — working alongside your engineers.

Ongoing Technical Stewardship

Lightweight retainers for teams that need continued architectural or maintenance oversight without full-time involvement. These can run for years if the relationship is a good fit.

Strategic Product Partnership

In limited cases, I am open to B2B partnerships or shared-IP arrangements where the problem is well-defined, high-impact, and aligns with my core interests in simulation and scientific computing.
