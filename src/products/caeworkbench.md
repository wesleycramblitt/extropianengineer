Extropian CAE Workbench is the application layer of the ecosystem: an extendable, GL-based workbench for configuring and running numerical solvers. It turns a powerful but developer-only solver into something engineers can drive — editing models and boundary conditions, managing batch processes, and saving and reloading scenes with reproducibility.

The workbench is the same interface technology in active use on the HyperFat hypersonic CFD engagement, and it is designed to be adapted to a different solver, mesh, or domain rather than rewritten.
